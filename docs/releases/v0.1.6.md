Trainlog v0.1.6 closes the Web refinement and synchronization-recovery cycle.
It improves factual Sleep and training presentation, preserves immutable
history through difficult replay and recovery cases, and gives the repository
a durable Catppuccin-based project entry point without widening frozen product
contracts.

## Local Web refinement

- Dashboard activity uses calendar months and ISO weeks, progression is a
  factual global synthesis, and Analyse drills from BODY ZONES to exercises and
  measured detail without inventing missing exercise time.
- A persistent French/English preference, Settings route, denser shared
  surfaces, responsive charts, and a shared BODY ZONES figure refine the local
  interface while presentation state remains outside business SQLite.
- Equipment administration can transactionally merge a custom duplicate into
  a canonical identity while preserving history, MAX context, preparations,
  Programs, and causal deletion evidence.

## Sleep facts and heart-rate timelines

- The multi-night agenda, selected-night heart-rate detail, and vector PDF use
  one temporal projection of factual sleep intervals, awakenings, point events,
  medication occurrences, and exact bounds.
- The shared 18:00-to-18:00 agenda keeps exact hourly geometry at desktop and
  narrow widths. A clearly labelled fallback estimate is presentation-only,
  non-medical, and never written to Sleep data.
- Android wake capture records revisioned long-awake intervals, medication
  quantity survives persistence and synchronization, and Sleep revision
  payloads are append-only on both platforms.

## Synchronization and recovery

- Bluetooth restart recovery retains immutable generation identity and accepts
  the exact original generation/digest acknowledgement across conversation
  runs; direct MTP remains the wired recovery transport.
- Android foreground and automatic synchronization share one ownership arbiter,
  and direct-MTP publication waits for MediaProvider visibility before exposing
  a mutable generation reference.
- Divergent Sleep revision identities can be recovered without choosing an
  arbitrary winner. Strict payload checks reject reuse of a historical revision
  identity with different parentage or content.
- A separate heart-rate correction companion removes samples strictly after a
  factual session end and prevents an older immutable generation from
  resurrecting the removed tail.

## Compatibility and validation

- Desktop schema v36 and Android schema v33 are additive migrations.
- `TRAINLOG_FORMAT_V1`, mobile export V3, stable identities, generation/ACK
  semantics, and Bluetooth/MTP transport ownership remain unchanged.
- The default automatic exchange remains V3; full-generation synchronization
  remains behind its trusted local opt-in.
- Native Meson and Clang ASan/UBSan suites each pass 114/114. Android passes
  317 tests (312 passed and five documented optional skips), debug assembly,
  signed release assembly, and signature verification. Web passes 32 files / 209
  tests plus typecheck/build; JSON/import validation, changed-C formatting, and
  diff checks also pass at the release tag.
